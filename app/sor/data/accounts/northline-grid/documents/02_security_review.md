# Security & Procurement Review

**Date:** 2026-08-06

Northline Grid Authority is reviewing the use of
Chinese-manufactured hardware within critical-infrastructure
deployments.

The security and procurement teams are evaluating whether
country-of-origin requirements could require changes to approved
hardware vendors.

The review may affect future deployment decisions and could
intersect with renewal planning.

The customer requested formal documentation covering:

- hardware sourcing
- data residency
- available hardware alternatives
- future hardware roadmap

No final procurement decision has been made.
