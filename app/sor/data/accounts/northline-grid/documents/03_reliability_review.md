# Quarterly Reliability Review

**Date:** 2026-07-30

Northline reported strong operational reliability during the
quarter.

A previous Gen2 dock experienced approximately 40 minutes of
downtime. The issue was resolved through a remote reboot and has
not recurred.

Gauge-reading missions have been performing reliably.

The customer also reported that a temporary decline in mission
activity was caused by regional snowfall rather than product
performance.

Northline continues to consider FlytBase operationally reliable.
